## Production notes

- **Pace is everything.** This is the 90-second cut — no lingering. Every screen beat is
  2–4 seconds. Cut on the action, not after it.
- **Scene 1** is the hook and the only scene allowed to breathe. The "record once / publish
  all year" text should land before any UI appears.
- **Scene 3** — use pre-generated results. Do not show a real generation wait.
- **Scene 4** — the aspect-ratio snap is the money shot; if you cut only one thing tight,
  don't cut this.
- **Never name a specific publishing platform in VO.** The Planner shows channel chips on
  screen, which is enough — see flag #1 in `00-verification-and-flags.md`.
- **Demo client only.** No real contact details, credentials, or email addresses on screen.
