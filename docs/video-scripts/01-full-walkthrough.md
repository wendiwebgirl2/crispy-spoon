# cue:cast — Full Walkthrough

**Use:** onboarding new staff · the cuecreative.com product page
**Runtime:** ~3:50 · **VO:** ~560 words at ~150 wpm
**Audience:** a new operator, or a prospect who wants to see the whole machine
**Scope:** only features confirmed shipped **and** deployed as of 2026-09-18 — see
`00-verification-and-flags.md`

**Brand:** brick red `#9c1b1f` · wood `#3a2114` · Helvetica Neue · lockup `cue:cast`

## Production notes

- **Demo client only.** Never show a real client's contact details, credentials, approval
  email addresses, or the send log's real recipients.
- **Scene 6** — generate against a topic that already has a saved generation type, so the
  preset auto-selects on screen rather than needing a manual click.
- **Scene 8** — the aspect ratio snapping when you pick "Short" is the strongest single
  beat in the video. Hold on it for a full second.
- **Scene 10** — pre-stitch an episode before the shoot. A real stitch takes minutes; cut
  to the finished result rather than waiting on a progress state.
- **Scene 11** — record the approve page in a second browser profile so the dashboard
  session stays logged in for the cut back.
- **Not in this script and not to be improvised on camera:** volume boost (disabled),
  Transistor by name (server config unconfirmed), voice-only casting (parked), episode
  multi-delete and burned-in captions (not built). See `00-verification-and-flags.md`.
